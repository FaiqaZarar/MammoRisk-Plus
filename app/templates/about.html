How each module works

{{ icon("mammogram") }} Classifier

Full mammograms, not cropped lesions, resized to about 1024 by 512. EfficientNet-B3 is compared with a ResNet-50 baseline on a patient-level split. The decision threshold is chosen on validation data and applied once to the test set. Heatmaps are scored against lesion outlines with the Pointing Game, energy-in-box and IoU, plus a weight-randomisation sanity check.

{{ icon("mirai") }} Mirai

The published Mirai model is used as it is, with no retraining. It reads the four views of one exam and gives a 1 to 5 year risk. Validation reports a C-index and time-dependent AUC with confidence intervals, split by breast density, race and machine vendor.

{{ icon("dna") }} PRS-313

Published weights (PGS000004) are applied to the genotype. The score is standardised against a European (CEU) and a pooled Pakistani reference, and we report how many people change risk category, with bootstrap intervals and the variants driving the shift.
