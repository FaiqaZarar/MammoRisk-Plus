Risk tools should say who they were tested on.

Breast cancer risk tools are built on Western and European women, then used without evidence that they classify Pakistani and South Asian women correctly. MammoRisk+ measures how far they can be trusted, and puts the answer on every result it shows.

What it does not claim

  • No diagnosis. This is a research prototype built on public datasets. Results must not be used to make decisions about any patient.
  • No verdict on the genetic categories. We show how a category shifts between references. Without cancer outcomes we cannot say which one is right, and we do not give absolute risk.
  • No result without its label. The software refuses to display or print a number that has no validation label.
