MammoRisk+

Breast cancer risk tools built on Western and European women are used without evidence that they classify Pakistani / South Asian women correctly. MammoRisk+ shows imaging and genetic risk results together with the datasets and populations each result was validated on.

  • Mammogram classifier

    Benign vs malignant probability and an explanation heatmap (Module 3).

  • Mirai risk

    1–5 year risk from the four standard views of one exam (Module 1).

  • PRS-313

    Genetic risk standardised against a European (CEU) vs Pakistani reference (Module 2).
